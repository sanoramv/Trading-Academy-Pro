# Market Journal Entry Template

Copy this per trade (paper or live). The point of journaling is honest,
structured self-review — not a trophy case of wins.

## Trade Header
- Date:
- Instrument:
- Market (Indian / US / Other):
- Direction (Long / Short):
- Timeframe:
- Paper Trade or Live:

## Pre-Trade Plan (fill BEFORE entry)
- Setup / Strategy used (link to `docs/24_Trading_Strategies/` lesson):
- Thesis (why this trade, in one paragraph):
- Entry Price / Trigger:
- Stop-Loss Price (and why there):
- Target Price(s) (and why there):
- Position Size and % Account Risk (link to `docs/26_Position_Sizing/`):
- Risk-Reward Ratio:
- Relevant macro/news context:

## Execution Notes (fill DURING the trade)
- Actual entry price/time:
- Did price behave as expected? Deviations?
- Any emotional impulses noticed (fear, greed, FOMO, revenge)?

## Post-Trade Review (fill AFTER exit)
- Exit price/time:
- Result (P&L, R-multiple):
- Was the original plan followed exactly? If not, what changed and why?
- What worked?
- What didn't work?
- Psychological grade (1–5) — did you follow your rules regardless of outcome?
- Lesson(s) this connects to:
- One change for next time:

---
*A good trade is one where the process was followed — not one that made
money. A bad trade is one where the process was broken — even if it made
money. Grade the process, not the P&L.*
