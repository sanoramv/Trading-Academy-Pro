# 90-Day Intermediate Plan

> Part of the [`STUDY_PLAN.md`](STUDY_PLAN.md) family. Read that file first for
> the shared day template, the assumptions behind the pacing, and how the
> four plans (30/90/180/365-day) relate to each other.

**Level:** **Intermediate.** You know the basics and want to go deeper: macroeconomics, how to read financial statements, how to value a company, and most of technical analysis — enough to read a chart and a balance sheet with real understanding.
**Duration:** 90 days (13 weeks)
**Weekday load:** 1 new lesson/day (~60-90 min including quiz & homework)
**Saturday load:** 2 new lessons (~144-170 min)
**Sunday:** Revision Day every week; Monthly Exam every 4th week — no new lessons, ever.
**Coverage:** `[01.01]` through `[10.07]` Momentum Oscillators — RSI, Stochastic, MACD — 89 of the 270 core lessons.
