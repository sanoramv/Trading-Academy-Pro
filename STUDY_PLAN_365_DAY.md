# 365-Day Mastery Plan

> Part of the [`STUDY_PLAN.md`](STUDY_PLAN.md) family. Read that file first for
> the shared day template, the assumptions behind the pacing, and how the
> four plans (30/90/180/365-day) relate to each other.

**Level:** **Mastery.** You want to complete the entire 270-lesson core curriculum — including risk management, portfolio construction, trading psychology, algorithmic and quantitative trading, and all 10 case studies — and then spend real time applying it: daily market analysis, spaced-repetition review, and capstone projects.
**Duration:** 365 days (53 weeks)
**Weekday load:** 1 new lesson/day (~60-90 min including quiz & homework)
**Saturday load:** 3 new lessons (~216-255 min)
**Sunday:** Revision Day every week; Monthly Exam every 4th week — no new lessons, ever.
**Coverage:** `[01.01]` through `[31.10]` Satyam Scandal (2009) — India's Enron — 270 of the 270 core lessons plus a Mastery/Practice phase once the core curriculum is finished (see below).
