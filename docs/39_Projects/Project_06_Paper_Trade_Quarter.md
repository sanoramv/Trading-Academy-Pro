# Project 6 — Paper-Trade a Full Quarter and Report Results

## Objective
Execute your Project 1 trading plan in a real (paper/simulated) market
for one full quarter, then produce an honest, data-driven performance
report — the ultimate integration test of this entire academy, closing
`[28.01]`'s knowing-doing gap with a real, sustained track record
rather than a one-off exercise.

## Requirements
1. Use your Project 1 trading plan exactly as written. If you don't have one yet, complete Project 1 first.
2. Paper-trade (simulated capital, real market conditions) for one full calendar quarter (minimum 10 weeks).
3. Journal every trade per `[24.12]`'s required fields, including explicit rule-adherence tracking.
4. At quarter-end, calculate: total return, win rate, average win/loss, expectancy (`[24.01]`), maximum drawdown (`[25.05]`), and Sharpe/Sortino ratio if you have enough data points (`[27.08]`).
5. Separately report performance for trades that followed your plan exactly versus trades that deviated — per `[28.07]`'s process-vs-outcome framework.
6. Write a final report answering: What worked? What didn't? Which specific psychological pattern (`[28.02]`-`[28.05]`) showed up most often? What ONE change would you make to your trading plan for next quarter?

## Deliverable Format
1. The completed trade journal (spreadsheet, all trades).
2. A final written report (2-3 pages) covering the required analysis above.

## Evaluation Criteria
- [ ] A genuine, minimum-10-week track record exists (not a backfilled or shortened period)
- [ ] Every trade includes the rule-adherence field, honestly reported
- [ ] Process-vs-outcome trades are separated and analyzed distinctly
- [ ] The final report identifies at least one SPECIFIC, real psychological pattern observed in yourself (not a generic acknowledgment that psychology matters)
- [ ] The report proposes one CONCRETE plan revision, not a vague intention

## Worked Example Fragment (Illustrative excerpt only)

```
QUARTER SUMMARY (10 weeks, N=34 trades):
  Overall: Win Rate 44%, Expectancy +0.28R, Max DD 11%

  RULE-ADHERENT trades (N=27): Win Rate 48%, Expectancy +0.41R
  RULE-DEVIATION trades (N=7): Win Rate 29%, Expectancy -0.35R

  OBSERVATION: "The 7 deviation trades, all occurring after a losing
  session, show a clear REVENGE TRADING pattern, [28.04] -- my
  overall expectancy would have been +0.41R (not +0.28R) had I
  followed my plan on every trade. CONCRETE CHANGE: adding a
  mandatory 24-hour cooling-off period after any 2-consecutive-loss
  session, before the next trade is permitted."
```
