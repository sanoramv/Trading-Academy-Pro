# Project 5 — Build a Macro Dashboard

## Objective
Apply Modules 04-06 and `[23.02]`-`[23.03]` to build a dashboard
tracking the macro/global context relevant to Indian markets — the
kind of daily-updated context `[32]`'s Daily Market Analysis template requires.

## Requirements
Your dashboard must track, updated regularly (daily or weekly):
1. **Global cues** — S&P 500/Nasdaq prior close, GIFT Nifty level (`[04.03]`)
2. **Volatility regime** — current India VIX and US VIX, plotted against their trailing 3-month range (`[23.02]`, `[23.03]`, `[23.05]`)
3. **Rates context** — current RBI repo rate and US Fed funds rate, with dates of next scheduled policy meetings (`[06.02]`)
4. **Currency/commodity** — USD/INR and crude oil price, both relevant to Indian market sentiment
5. **Yield curve snapshot** — India and/or US yield curve shape (normal/flat/inverted) (`[06.07]`)
6. **A written, one-paragraph "regime summary"** updated each time you refresh the dashboard — is this a risk-on or risk-off environment, and why?

## Deliverable Format
A spreadsheet or simple document updated on a regular cadence (commit
to daily or weekly) for at least 2 weeks, showing the tracked history,
not just a single snapshot.

## Evaluation Criteria
- [ ] All six required sections are present
- [ ] The dashboard was genuinely updated across multiple sessions (showing a real history), not filled in once
- [ ] The "regime summary" reflects actual synthesis of the data, not a generic statement
- [ ] You can point to at least one instance where your regime read informed (or would have informed) an actual trading/position decision
