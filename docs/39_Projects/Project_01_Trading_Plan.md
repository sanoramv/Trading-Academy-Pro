# Project 1 — Build a Complete Trading Plan

## Objective
Synthesize Modules 24-28 into one written, personal, specific trading
plan you would actually follow — closing `[28.01]`'s knowing-doing gap
through a real, concrete artifact rather than abstract understanding.

## Requirements
Your trading plan must include, at minimum:
1. **Strategy section** — name the specific Module 24 framework(s) you'll trade, with exact entry/exit criteria written out (not "buy when it looks good").
2. **Risk management section** — per `[25.08]`'s template: risk-per-trade limit, stop-loss methodology, minimum risk-reward, max drawdown response, correlation/cluster limits, tail-risk safeguards.
3. **Position sizing section** — the specific formula (fixed fractional, ATR-based, etc.) and worked calculation examples.
4. **Psychological circuit breakers** — specific, numbered triggers for tilt (`[28.04]`), FOMO (`[28.05]`), and at least one bias countermeasure (`[28.03]`).
5. **Review schedule** — how often, and using what criteria (`[24.12]`), you'll revise this plan.

## Deliverable Format
A single markdown or document file, using `[25.08]`'s and `[28.06]`'s templates as your structural guide. Minimum 2 pages.

## Evaluation Criteria
- [ ] Every rule is SPECIFIC and checkable (a number, not a vague intention)
- [ ] Entry/exit criteria reference an actual Module 24 framework by name
- [ ] Risk section includes all six `[25.08]` components
- [ ] At least one psychological circuit breaker is genuinely personal (based on your own `[38]` Drill 8 self-audit)
- [ ] A stranger reading this plan could understand exactly when you would and wouldn't enter a trade

## Worked Example Fragment (Illustrative excerpt only)
```
STRATEGY: Trend-following, [24.02], on Nifty and 3 pre-selected large-cap stocks
ENTRY: Price closes above rising 50-day EMA; wait for pullback to
       20-day EMA; enter on a bullish engulfing candle at that level
STOP: Below the pullback's low (structural, [25.03])
SIZE: 1% fixed fractional, [26.02]
TILT TRIGGER: 2 consecutive losses in a session -> stop trading for the day
```
