# Flashcard Deck 10 — Case Studies

> Covers Module 31 (Case Studies). Part of Module 34.

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| What triggered the 2008 Global Financial Crisis? | The collapse of the US subprime mortgage market. `[31.01]` |
| Approximately how large was the S&P 500's peak-to-trough decline in the GFC? | Approximately 57%. `[31.01]` |
| What made the 2020 COVID crash's recovery unusual? | Its V-shape — new all-time highs within 6-12 months, far faster than the GFC's multi-year recovery. `[31.02]` |
| What mechanism did Harshad Mehta exploit? | Fraudulent Bank Receipts diverting bank settlement funds into leveraged stock purchases. `[31.03]` |
| What mechanism did Ketan Parekh use to manipulate prices? | Circular trading among connected entities in relatively illiquid, mid-cap "K-10" stocks. `[31.04]` |
| What triggered the 2004 Indian election result crash? | An unexpected election result raising uncertainty about future economic policy direction. `[31.05]` |
| What triggered the Adani Group stock decline examined in Lesson 31.06? | A Hindenburg Research report (January 2023) alleging accounting and governance irregularities. `[31.06]` |
| What is a short squeeze? | A heavily shorted stock's rising price forcing short covering, adding further upward pressure. `[31.07]` |
| What social media platform substantially coordinated GameStop retail buying? | r/WallStreetBets (Reddit). `[31.07]` |
| What two Nobel laureates were LTCM partners? | Myron Scholes and Robert Merton. `[31.08]` |
| What event triggered LTCM's collapse? | Russia's August 1998 sovereign debt default and currency devaluation. `[31.08]` |
| Approximately how high did the NASDAQ Composite peak in March 2000? | Approximately 5,048. `[31.09]` |
| Approximately how much did NASDAQ fall from its 2000 peak to its 2002 trough? | Roughly 78%, to around 1,114 by October 2002. `[31.09]` |
| What did Ramalinga Raju confess to in January 2009? | A multi-year accounting fraud fabricating Satyam's revenue, profit, and cash balances. `[31.10]` |
| Why is Satyam often called "India's Enron"? | It directly parallels the US Enron scandal's mechanism and regulatory aftermath. `[31.10]` |
| What regulatory reform followed the Harshad Mehta scam? | SEBI's statutory powers expanded, and NSE was founded (1992) with modern electronic settlement. `[31.03]` |
| What was the fundamental cause of LTCM's failure? | Excessive leverage relative to genuine tail risk, not a flaw in the underlying quantitative strategies. `[31.08]` |
| What real event illustrates undefined-risk options sizing failure? | Volmageddon (February 2018). `[22.08]`, `[26.05]` |
| What is the common thread across most of this module's ten case studies? | The disproportionate consequences of inadequate risk management, leverage, or governance discipline. Module 31 synthesis |
| Which two Indian scandals in this module involve settlement/price-manipulation, versus which one involves direct accounting fraud? | Harshad Mehta (settlement fraud) and Ketan Parekh (price manipulation) vs. Satyam (accounting fraud). `[31.03]`, `[31.04]`, `[31.10]` |
