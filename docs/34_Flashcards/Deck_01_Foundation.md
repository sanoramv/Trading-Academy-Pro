# Flashcard Deck 1 — Foundation

> Covers Modules 01 (Foundation), 02 (Market Structure), 07 (Financial Statements),
> 08 (Fundamental Analysis), 09 (Valuation). Part of Module 34.

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| What is the difference between a market and an exchange? | A market is the broader system of buyers/sellers; an exchange is the organized venue facilitating trade. `[01.01]` |
| What distinguishes trading from investing? | Time horizon and decision basis — trading is shorter-term and technical/price-driven; investing is longer-term and fundamentals-driven. `[01.02]` |
| What is price-time priority? | The order matching rule where better prices execute first, and among equal prices, earlier orders execute first. `[01.04]` |
| What are the three main financial statements? | The Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement. `[07.01]` |
| What does the Income Statement show? | A company's revenue, expenses, and profit over a period. `[07.02]` |
| What does the Balance Sheet show? | A company's assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time. `[07.03]` |
| What is market structure? | The pattern of highs, lows, and trend/range behavior a price chart forms over time. `[02.01]` |
| What defines an uptrend in classical structure terms? | A sequence of higher highs and higher lows. `[02.03]` |
| What is the difference between BOS and CHOCH? | BOS confirms trend continuation; CHOCH warns of a potential trend reversal. `[02.05]` |
| What is liquidity in a market structure context? | Clusters of resting orders (stops, pending orders) that price is drawn toward. `[02.06]` |
| What is fundamental analysis? | Evaluating a security's intrinsic value using financial statements, industry, and macro context. `[08.01]` |
| What is the difference between top-down and bottom-up analysis? | Top-down starts from macro/sector context down to the company; bottom-up starts from the company outward. `[08.02]` |
| What does P/E ratio measure? | Price relative to earnings per share — a common relative valuation multiple. `[08.06]`, `[09.02]` |
| What is a competitive moat? | A sustainable structural advantage protecting a company's profits from competition. `[08.07]` |
| What is DCF (Discounted Cash Flow)? | A valuation method estimating intrinsic value from projected future cash flows discounted to present value. `[09.03]` |
| What is a valuation trap? | A stock that appears cheap on simple multiples but is cheap for a persistent, structural reason. `[09.06]` |
| What are the four broad asset classes covered in Module 01? | Equity, debt, currency, and commodities (plus derivatives as a fifth category). `[01.03]` |
| What is the role of a clearing corporation? | Guaranteeing trade settlement and managing counterparty risk between buyers and sellers. `[01.09]` |
| What is T+1 settlement? | The Indian market convention where trades settle one business day after execution. `[03.06]` |
| What is a swing point/fractal? | A formal, rule-based local high or low used to define market structure. `[02.07]` |
