# Flashcard Deck 2 — Indian Market

> Covers Module 03 (Indian Market). Part of Module 34.

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| When was BSE established, and when did NSE launch? | BSE in 1875; NSE in 1992. `[03.01]` |
| What is SEBI's core mandate? | To regulate, develop, and protect investors in Indian securities markets. `[03.02]` |
| How are Nifty 50 and Sensex constructed? | Using free-float market capitalization weighting. `[03.04]` |
| What is T+1 settlement, and when did it apply broadly in India? | Trades settling one business day after execution; broadly implemented across Indian equities in recent years. `[03.06]` |
| What is the difference between an FII and a DII? | FIIs are foreign institutional investors; DIIs are domestic institutional investors (mutual funds, insurers). `[03.07]` |
| What does F&O stand for? | Futures and Options — India's derivatives market segment. `[03.08]` |
| What is a circuit filter? | A price-band mechanism halting trading when a stock/index moves beyond a set percentage in a session. `[03.05]` |
| What historical event directly catalyzed NSE's founding? | The 1992 Harshad Mehta scam, which exposed settlement infrastructure vulnerabilities. `[03.01]`, `[31.03]` |
| What are NSDL and CDSL? | India's two central securities depositories, holding shares in electronic (dematerialized) form. `[01.09]` |
| What is insider trading, per SEBI regulation? | Trading based on unpublished price-sensitive information (UPSI) not available to the public. `[03.09]` |
| How does the Union Budget typically affect Indian markets? | Through changes to taxation, spending, and policy signals that shift sector and macro expectations. `[03.10]` |
| What is NSCCL? | NSE's clearing corporation, guaranteeing trade settlement. `[03.06]` |
| What distinguishes NSE from BSE structurally? | NSE pioneered fully electronic, screen-based trading in 1994; both now operate electronically but differ in index composition and trading volume concentration. `[03.03]` |
| What is India VIX? | NSE's index of expected Nifty volatility over the next 30 days. `[23.02]` |
| What was the core mechanism of the Harshad Mehta scam? | Fraudulent Bank Receipts diverting bank settlement funds into leveraged stock purchases. `[31.03]` |
| What was the core mechanism of the Ketan Parekh scam? | Circular trading among connected entities manipulating prices of illiquid, mid-cap stocks. `[31.04]` |
| What triggered the 2004 Indian election result crash? | An unexpected election result raising uncertainty about future economic policy direction. `[31.05]` |
| What was the Satyam scandal? | A multi-year accounting fraud, publicly confessed by founder Ramalinga Raju in January 2009, often called "India's Enron." `[31.10]` |
| What is SPAN margining? | An exchange-standard, worst-case-scenario-based margin calculation methodology used by NSE and other exchanges. `[26.05]` |
| What is the Indian market's regular trading session timing? | Approximately 9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST, with a pre-open session beforehand. `[03.05]` |
