|---|---|
| What is GIFT Nifty/SGX Nifty used for? | Gauging likely Nifty opening direction based on overnight global cues. `[04.03]` |
| What is a central bank's core policy tool? | Setting interest rates (e.g., repo rate for RBI, federal funds rate for the Fed). `[06.02]` |
| What is quantitative easing (QE)? | A central bank policy of large-scale asset purchases to inject liquidity and lower long-term rates. `[06.03]` |
| What does an inverted yield curve typically signal? | A widely watched, though imperfect, recession indicator. `[06.07]` |
| What is inflation? | A sustained rise in the general price level of goods and services over time. `[05.03]` |
| What is GDP? | The total value of goods and services produced by an economy over a period. `[05.05]` |
| What is the difference between fiscal and monetary policy? | Fiscal policy (government spending/taxation) vs. monetary policy (central bank interest rates/money supply). `[05.07]` |
| What are the four business cycle stages? | Expansion, peak, contraction (recession), and trough. `[05.08]` |
| What is India's inflation-targeting framework? | RBI's mandate to keep CPI inflation within a specified target band. `[06.10]` |
| What is risk-on/risk-off? | A market regime describing whether capital is flowing toward higher-risk or safer assets. `[06.06]` |
| What is the balance of payments? | A country's record of all economic transactions with the rest of the world. `[06.05]` |
| What does "correlation between global markets" refer to? | The tendency for major markets (US, Europe, Asia, India) to move together, especially during risk-off episodes. `[04.06]` |
| What distinguishes emerging markets from developed markets? | Emerging markets typically show higher growth potential alongside higher volatility and structural risk. `[04.07]` |
| What is a commodity super-cycle? | An extended period of rising commodity prices driven by structural supply/demand shifts. `[06.08]` |
| Who won the 2017 Nobel Prize partly for overreaction/behavioral finance research relevant to macro cycles? | Richard Thaler (De Bondt-Thaler long-term reversion research). `[30.04]` |
| What is the Fed's dual mandate? | Maximum employment and price stability. `[06.01]` (referenced) |
| What is currency devaluation? | A deliberate or market-driven reduction in a currency's value relative to others. `[06.04]` |
| What major global event triggered the 2008 GFC? | The collapse of the US subprime mortgage market. `[31.01]` |
| What made the 2020 COVID crash's recovery unusual? | Its V-shape — new all-time highs within 6-12 months, far faster than the GFC's multi-year recovery. `[31.02]` |
