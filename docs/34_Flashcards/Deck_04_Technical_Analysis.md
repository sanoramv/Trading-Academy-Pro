# Flashcard Deck 4 — Technical Analysis

> Covers Modules 10 (Technical Analysis), 11 (Candlesticks), 12 (Chart
> Patterns), 13 (Price Action), 14 (Volume), 15-16 (Market/Volume
> Profile). Part of Module 34.

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| What are the three tenets of Dow Theory relevant to this academy? | Price discounts everything, markets move in trends, and trends persist until a clear reversal signal. `[10.02]` |
| What is the difference between SMA and EMA? | SMA weights all periods equally; EMA weights recent prices more heavily. `[10.06]` |
| What does RSI measure? | The speed and magnitude of recent price changes, identifying overbought/oversold conditions. `[10.07]` |
| What is a bullish engulfing pattern? | A two-candle pattern where a larger bullish candle fully engulfs the prior bearish candle's body. `[11.04]` |
| What is a Head and Shoulders pattern? | A reversal chart pattern with three peaks, the middle one highest, signaling a trend change. `[12.01]` |
| What is the difference between a breakout and a fakeout? | A breakout is a genuine, sustained move beyond a level; a fakeout reverses back inside shortly after. `[13.04]` |
| What confirms a genuine breakout? | Above-average volume alongside a genuine close beyond the boundary. `[14.03]`, `[24.04]` |
| What is Point of Control (POC)? | The price level with the highest traded volume within a profile period. `[16.02]` |
| What is Value Area in Market/Volume Profile? | The price range containing a specified percentage (commonly 70%) of a session's traded volume. `[15.02]` |
| What is climactic volume? | Unusually high volume signaling potential exhaustion of a move. `[14.05]` |
| What is a doji candlestick? | A candle with a very small body, indicating indecision between buyers and sellers. `[11.02]` |
| What is the "Initial Balance" in Market Profile? | The price range established during the first hour of trading, used to gauge intraday bias. `[15.04]` |
| What is regular vs. hidden divergence? | Regular divergence signals potential reversal; hidden divergence signals potential trend continuation. `[10.10]` |
| What is a cup and handle pattern? | A bullish continuation pattern resembling a rounded bottom followed by a small pullback. `[12.06]` |
| What does "location matters more than shape" mean for candlestick patterns? | A pattern's reliability depends heavily on where it occurs (at a key level) more than its exact shape. `[11.08]` |
| What is the difference between Market Profile and Volume Profile? | Market Profile uses time-at-price (TPO); Volume Profile uses actual traded volume-at-price. `[16.04]` |
| What is a fractal/swing point? | A formal, rule-based local high or low point. `[02.07]` |
| What is the Wyckoff-adjacent concept of "Composite Man"? | A mental model treating the aggregate of large institutional participants as a single, deliberate actor. `[17.06]` |
| What is a fair value gap (FVG)? | A price imbalance left by a rapid move, often revisited later. `[18.04]` |
| What does volume confirm in technical analysis? | Whether a price move reflects genuine, broad participation or thin, less reliable activity. `[14.01]` |
