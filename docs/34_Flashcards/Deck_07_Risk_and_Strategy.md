# Flashcard Deck 7 — Strategy, Risk, Sizing & Portfolio

> Covers Modules 24 (Trading Strategies), 25 (Risk Management), 26
> (Position Sizing), 27 (Portfolio Management). Part of Module 34.

| Front | Back |
|---|---|
| What is the expectancy formula? | (Win Rate × Avg Win) − (Loss Rate × Avg Loss). `[24.01]` |
| What win rate pattern is typical for trend-following? | Low win rate, offset by large average wins. `[24.02]` |
| What win rate pattern is typical for mean-reversion? | Higher win rate, smaller average wins. `[24.03]` |
| What is the "1% rule"? | The guideline of risking no more than 1% of trading capital on any single trade's stop-loss distance. `[25.02]` |
| What determines a structural stop's placement? | The price level where the trade's original thesis is invalidated. `[25.03]` |
| What is the breakeven win rate formula? | Risk / (Risk + Reward). `[25.04]` |
| What is maximum drawdown? | The largest peak-to-trough equity decline observed in a track record. `[25.05]` |
| What is correlation risk? | The risk that multiple individually-sized positions move together, producing combined losses beyond individual limits. `[25.06]` |
| What is a black swan event? | A rare, extreme, hard-to-predict event, often rationalized as predictable only in hindsight. `[25.07]` |
| What is fixed fractional position sizing? | Risking a constant percentage of CURRENT account equity on every trade. `[26.02]` |
| What is the Kelly Criterion formula? | Kelly % = W − [(1−W)/R], where W = win rate, R = payoff ratio. `[26.04]` |
| What distinguishes defined-risk from undefined-risk options positions? | Defined-risk has a known, fixed maximum loss; undefined-risk (naked short options) does not. `[26.05]` |
| What is scaling in? | Entering a position across multiple tranches, typically adding on confirmation of the thesis. `[26.06]` |
| What is systematic risk? | Broad, market-wide risk affecting nearly all assets — cannot be diversified away. `[27.01]` |
| What did Brinson, Hood, and Beebower's research find? | Asset allocation policy explained the majority of return variability for the pension funds studied. `[27.02]` |
| What is MPT's core insight? | Combining imperfectly correlated assets can reduce portfolio risk without necessarily sacrificing return. `[27.03]` |
| What is rebalancing? | Restoring a portfolio's actual allocation back to its intended target weights. `[27.05]` |
| What is a core-satellite portfolio? | A large, diversified, passive core combined with smaller, bounded, active satellites. `[27.07]` |
| What does the Sharpe ratio measure? | Excess return per unit of total risk (volatility). `[27.08]` |
| What is event-driven trading's three-way decision framework? | Directional bet, volatility bet, or standing aside — never trading through an event unprepared. `[24.10]` |
