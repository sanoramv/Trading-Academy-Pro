# Module 11 — Candlesticks

> Module 10 introduced the candlestick chart type. This module goes deep
> on reading individual and multi-candle patterns — the specific,
> named formations traders have used for centuries to read short-term
> buyer/seller psychology directly from price bars.

## Why This Module Exists
Candlestick patterns are among the most widely taught, and most widely
misused, tools in trading education — often presented as standalone
magic signals disconnected from context. This module teaches them
correctly: as a vocabulary for reading price psychology that only means
something in the context of Module 02's market structure, never in
isolation.
