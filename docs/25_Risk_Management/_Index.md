# Module 25 — Risk Management

> Module 24 built strategy frameworks assuming disciplined position
> sizing and stop-losses without fully specifying either. This module
> makes that discipline explicit — the risk management rules every
> strategy in this academy depends on to survive long enough for its
> expectancy to play out.

## Why This Module Exists
Per `MASTER_SKILL.md`'s core teaching rule, risk management is taught
before returns throughout this academy — this module is where that
principle gets its full, dedicated treatment, covering per-trade risk
definition, stop-loss methodology, risk-reward math, drawdown, and
tail-risk awareness.
