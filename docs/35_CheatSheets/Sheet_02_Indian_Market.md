## Settlement & Timing (`[03.05]`, `[03.06]`)
- Settlement cycle: T+1
- Regular session: ~9:15 AM – 3:30 PM IST
- Circuit filters: price bands halting trading at set % moves

## FII/DII Quick Reference (`[03.07]`)
- FII = Foreign Institutional Investor
- DII = Domestic Institutional Investor (mutual funds, insurers)
- SIP-driven DII flows have historically provided resilience against FII outflow volatility
