## Recurring Risk Patterns Across All Ten Events
```
1. LEVERAGE amplifies both gains AND losses (GFC, LTCM)
2. CORRELATION rises toward 1 during genuine crises (GFC, LTCM)
3. GOVERNANCE/disclosure failures precede severe repricing
   (Satyam, Adani, Enron parallel)
4. UNCERTAINTY itself (not just bad news) can drive sharp reactions
   (2004 election crash)
5. UNDEFINED RISK (short positions) can compound losses without limit
   (GameStop)
6. RECOVERY SPEED varies dramatically by crisis TYPE
   (COVID: 6-12mo vs. GFC: 4+ years)
```
