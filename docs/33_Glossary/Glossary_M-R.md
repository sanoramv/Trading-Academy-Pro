# Glossary — M to R

> Part of Module 33. See `_Index.md` for how to use this reference.

**Margin** — Collateral required to hold a leveraged position (futures, short options). `[20.04]`

**Market Impact** — The tendency for a large order to move price unfavorably against the trader as it executes. `[29.05]`

**Market Maker** — A participant continuously quoting bid and ask prices, profiting from the spread. `[01.05]`, `[30.06]`

**Market Structure** — The pattern of highs, lows, and trend/range behavior a price chart forms. `[02.01]`

**Maximum Drawdown (MDD)** — The largest peak-to-trough equity decline observed in a track record. `[25.05]`

**Mean Reversion** — A strategy betting a stretched price move within a genuine range will revert toward the center. `[24.03]`

**Momentum** — The tendency for an asset's recent price performance to continue in the same direction. `[24.08]`, `[30.04]`

**Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT)** — Markowitz's framework for constructing portfolios maximizing return for a given risk level. `[27.03]`

**Moving Average** — A trend-following indicator averaging price over a set lookback period. `[10.06]`

**Nifty 50** — NSE's benchmark index of the 50 largest, most liquid Indian stocks. `[03.04]`

**Order Block** — An SMC concept marking the last opposing candle before a significant, decisive price move. `[18.03]`

**Overfitting (Curve-Fitting)** — Tuning a strategy too precisely to historical data's noise rather than a genuine, persistent pattern. `[29.04]`

**Pairs Trading** — A market-neutral strategy betting on the reversion of a cointegrated spread between two instruments. `[30.03]`

**PCR (Put-Call Ratio)** — Total put open interest divided by total call open interest; a sentiment gauge. `[21.11]`

**Portfolio** — A collection of holdings managed together as a single, coherent unit. `[27.01]`

**Position Sizing** — The formula converting a risk-per-trade principle into an actual number of units/contracts to trade. `[26.01]`

**Premium (Options)** — The price paid to buy, or received to sell, an option contract. `[21.03]`

**Put Option** — A contract giving the buyer the right, not obligation, to sell an asset at a set strike price. `[21.01]`

**Quantitative Trading** — Deriving trading strategy ideas from rigorous statistical/mathematical analysis of data. `[30.01]`

**Recency Bias** — The tendency to weight recent events more heavily than earlier ones when assessing patterns. `[28.03]`

**Relative Strength Index (RSI)** — A momentum oscillator measuring the speed and magnitude of recent price changes. `[10.07]`

**Renaissance Technologies** — A quant hedge fund founded 1982 by James Simons, exemplifying purely statistical trading. `[30.01]`

**Revenge Trading** — An impulsive, often oversized attempt to immediately recover a recent loss, a symptom of tilt. `[28.04]`

**Rho** — The Greek measuring an option's premium sensitivity to interest rate changes. `[22.06]`

**Risk-Free Rate** — The theoretical return of an investment with zero risk, used as a baseline in Sharpe/CAPM calculations. `[27.08]`

**Risk-Reward Ratio** — The ratio of a trade's risk (entry to stop) to its reward (entry to target). `[25.04]`

**Risk-Per-Trade** — The amount of capital that will actually be lost if a position's stop-loss is hit. `[25.02]`
