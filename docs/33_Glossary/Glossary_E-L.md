# Glossary — E to L

> Part of Module 33. See `_Index.md` for how to use this reference.

**Efficient Frontier** — The set of portfolios offering maximum return for each level of risk, per Modern Portfolio Theory. `[27.03]`

**Elliott Wave** — A pattern-based theory of market cycles built on fractal wave structures. `[02.07]` (referenced)

**EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** — A moving average weighting recent prices more heavily than older ones. `[10.06]`

**Execution Algorithm** — A system (TWAP, VWAP, Iceberg) managing how a large order is placed to minimize market impact. `[29.05]`

**Expectancy** — The average expected profit per trade, combining win rate and average win/loss size. `[24.01]`

**Factor (Quant)** — A systematic, measurable characteristic (value, momentum, quality, low-vol) explaining return differences across stocks. `[30.05]`

**Factor Investing** — Systematically tilting a portfolio toward stocks scoring highly on a chosen factor. `[30.05]`

**Fair Value Gap (FVG)** — An SMC/ICT concept describing a price imbalance left by a rapid move. `[18.04]`

**Fat Tails** — The tendency for real market returns to produce extreme outcomes more often than a normal distribution predicts. `[25.07]`, `[30.02]`

**Fear and Greed Cycle** — The recurring emotional pattern driving market sentiment through euphoria near tops and despondency near bottoms. `[28.02]`

**FII (Foreign Institutional Investor)** — A foreign entity investing in Indian markets. `[03.07]`

**Fixed Fractional Sizing** — Risking a constant percentage of current account equity on every trade. `[26.02]`

**FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)** — The anxious urge to enter a position based on fear of missing a move already in progress. `[28.05]`

**Fundamental Analysis** — Evaluating a security's intrinsic value from financial statements, industry, and macro context. `[08.01]`

**Futures Contract** — A standardized agreement to buy/sell an asset at a set price on a future date. `[20.01]`

**Gamma** — The Greek measuring the rate of change of Delta itself. `[22.03]`

**Gap** — A price discontinuity where an asset opens meaningfully above or below its prior close. `[24.05]`

**GDP (Gross Domestic Product)** — The total value of goods and services produced by an economy. `[05.05]`

**Greeks** — The set of sensitivity measures (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Rho) describing an option's price sensitivity to each input. `[22.01]`

**HFT (High-Frequency Trading)** — An extreme-speed category of algorithmic trading competing on microsecond execution. `[29.08]`

**Hindenburg Research** — A short-seller research firm; publisher of the January 2023 report on the Adani Group. `[31.06]`

**Iceberg Order** — An order displaying only a small visible portion of a much larger total order. `[29.05]`

**ICT (Inner Circle Trader)** — A trading methodology emphasizing market maker models, kill zones, and liquidity concepts. `[19.01]`

**Implied Volatility (IV)** — Volatility reverse-engineered from an option's current market price, representing the market's forward-looking expectation. `[23.01]`

**India VIX** — NSE's index of expected Nifty volatility over the next 30 days. `[23.02]`

**Inventory Risk** — A market maker's risk of accumulated, unhedged directional exposure from asymmetric order flow. `[30.06]`

**Iron Condor** — A four-leg, defined-risk options strategy profiting when the underlying stays within a range. `[21.10]`

**Kelly Criterion** — A formula deriving the position size that maximizes long-run geometric growth, given win rate and payoff ratio. `[26.04]`

**Kurtosis** — A statistic measuring the "fatness" of a distribution's tails relative to a normal distribution. `[30.02]`

**Leverage** — Using borrowed capital to amplify a position's exposure beyond the trader's own capital. `[20.04]`

**Liquidity** — The ease with which an asset can be bought or sold without significantly affecting its price. `[02.06]`

**Long-Term Capital Management (LTCM)** — A hedge fund whose 1998 collapse illustrates leverage and correlation-breakdown risk. `[31.08]`

**Look-Ahead Bias** — A backtesting pitfall of using information that wouldn't have been available at the actual simulated trade moment. `[29.03]`

**Loss Aversion** — The tendency for losses to be felt more intensely than equivalent gains, per Prospect Theory. `[28.03]`
