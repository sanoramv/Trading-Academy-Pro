# Glossary — A to D

> Part of Module 33. See `_Index.md` for how to use this reference.

**Alpha** — Return generated beyond what a portfolio's Beta/market exposure alone would predict; a proxy for skill-based outperformance. `[27.08]`

**Asset Allocation** — The decision of how much capital to place across broad asset classes (equity, debt, cash, etc.), distinct from security selection. `[27.02]`

**Asset Class** — A broad category of investable instrument (equity, debt, currency, commodities, derivatives). `[01.03]`

**ATR (Average True Range)** — A volatility measure of an instrument's typical price range, used for stop placement and position sizing. `[25.03]`, `[26.03]`

**Backtesting** — Testing a strategy's rules against historical data to estimate performance. `[29.03]`

**Bank Receipt (BR)** — A document representing government securities held on a bank's behalf; central to the Harshad Mehta scam's mechanism. `[31.03]`

**Bear Put Spread** — A defined-risk options strategy buying a higher-strike put and selling a lower-strike put, betting on a decline. `[21.08]`

**Beta** — A measure of a stock or portfolio's sensitivity to overall market movement (systematic risk). `[27.08]`

**Bid-Ask Spread** — The difference between the highest price a buyer will pay (bid) and the lowest price a seller will accept (ask); a market maker's primary profit source. `[01.10]`, `[30.06]`

**Black Swan Event** — A rare, extreme, hard-to-predict event, often rationalized as predictable only in hindsight (Taleb). `[25.07]`

**Black-Scholes Model** — The 1973 option pricing model underlying modern Greeks and derivatives pricing. `[21.04]`, `[22.01]`

**Bollinger Bands** — (referenced conceptually) A volatility-based indicator plotting bands around a moving average.

**Bond** — A debt instrument representing a loan from investor to issuer. `[01.03]`

**BOS (Break of Structure)** — A trend-continuation price break through a prior swing point. `[02.05]`

**Breakout** — A decisive price move beyond an established range boundary. `[13.04]`, `[24.04]`

**Bull Call Spread** — A defined-risk options strategy buying a lower-strike call and selling a higher-strike call, betting on a rise. `[21.08]`

**Business Cycle** — The recurring pattern of economic expansion, peak, contraction, and trough. `[05.08]`

**Call Option** — A contract giving the buyer the right, not obligation, to buy an asset at a set strike price. `[21.01]`

**CAPM (Capital Asset Pricing Model)** — Sharpe's model relating expected return to systematic risk (Beta), extending Markowitz's MPT. `[27.08]`

**Circuit Breaker / Circuit Filter** — An exchange mechanism halting trading when price moves exceed a predefined threshold. `[03.05]`

**Cointegration** — A statistical property where a linear combination of two instruments' price levels reverts to a stable long-run mean. `[30.03]`

**Confirmation Bias** — The tendency to seek information confirming an existing belief while discounting contradicting evidence. `[28.03]`

**Contango** — A futures market condition where futures prices exceed the spot price. `[20.05]`

**Core-Satellite Portfolio** — A structure combining a large, diversified passive core with smaller, bounded active satellites. `[27.07]`

**Correlation** — A statistical measure of how two assets' returns move together. `[25.06]`, `[27.04]`

**Correlation Risk** — The risk that multiple individually-sized positions move together, producing combined losses beyond individual limits. `[25.06]`

**Covered Call** — A strategy combining long stock with a short call against it. `[21.07]`

**CTA (Commodity Trading Advisor)** — An institutional systematic trend-following fund manager. `[24.02]`

**Delta** — The Greek measuring an option's premium sensitivity to a 1-point move in the underlying. `[22.02]`

**Diversification** — Holding multiple, genuinely uncorrelated assets to reduce unsystematic risk without necessarily sacrificing return. `[27.01]`

**Dividend** — A cash or stock distribution paid by a company to shareholders.

**Dow Theory** — Charles Dow's foundational technical analysis framework. `[10.02]`

**Drawdown** — The decline from an equity curve's peak to its subsequent trough. `[25.05]`

**DMA (Direct Market Access)** — Institutional infrastructure permitting direct order routing to exchanges without manual broker intervention. `[29.01]`, `[29.07]`
