# Module 21 — Options

> Module 20 built your understanding of futures — leveraged, linear
> instruments with symmetric risk/reward. This module introduces
> options: instruments with fundamentally asymmetric, non-linear payoff
> structures that require a distinct mental model.

## Why This Module Exists
Options are the most widely-traded derivative instrument among Indian
retail traders (`[03.08]`'s F&O overview), yet also among the most
commonly misunderstood — this module builds a rigorous foundation from
first principles (calls, puts, premium, moneyness) through practical
payoff diagrams and common strategies, before Module 22 introduces the
Greeks that govern option pricing sensitivity.
