## Purpose
This exam targets students pursuing professional trading or a
finance-industry career track (institutional desks, `[30.08]`'s career
landscape) — questions emphasize institutional perspective, precise
mechanism explanation, and defending judgment calls under scrutiny, not
just correct recall.

## Section A — Recall (5 questions)
1. What is the institutional equivalent of a retail trader's personal risk rulebook? `[25.08]`, `[28.08]`
2. What formal risk metric do institutions pair with stress testing, and why is the pairing necessary? `[30.07]`
3. What is SPAN margining? `[26.05]`
4. What academic finding underlies institutional factor investing? `[30.05]`
5. What historical event most directly shaped Dodd-Frank-style leverage regulation? `[31.01]`

## Section B — Integrated Application (10 questions)
6. Explain, mechanistically, how LTCM's Nobel Prize-winning models still failed — what specifically broke down, and why did expertise not prevent it?
7. Design an institutional-style risk gate for an automated strategy (`[29.06]`) — what would it check, and why must it be architecturally independent from signal logic?
8. A junior analyst proposes a factor strategy with an impressive backtest. As a risk manager, what three questions would you ask before approving capital allocation?
9. Explain the difference between systematic and unsystematic risk using a real, non-textbook example of your own construction.
10. A market maker's inventory risk spikes during a volatile session. Explain the specific hedging mechanism they'd likely deploy.
11. Compare the 2008 GFC's and 2020 COVID crash's policy responses — what specifically explains the difference in recovery speed?
12. Explain cointegration precisely enough that a colleague unfamiliar with the term could distinguish it from correlation without looking it up.
13. A pairs trade's cointegration relationship appears to be breaking down. What would you check before deciding whether to exit?
14. Explain the volatility risk premium and its relevance to institutional theta-selling desks.
15. A trader proposes sizing a position using full Kelly. As a risk manager, what would you require before approving this, if anything?

## Section C — Judgment (5 questions)
16. Defend or critique: "Quantitative sophistication is sufficient risk management on its own." Use LTCM as your primary evidence.
17. A junior trader on your desk is showing signs of tilt after a loss. What structural (not just personal) intervention would you implement?
18. Evaluate SMC/ICT frameworks' testability criticisms from a professional risk perspective — does using an untestable-but-intuitive framework change how you'd size positions?
19. Explain why "the strategy backtested well" is necessary but not sufficient for institutional capital allocation.
20. Articulate, as if briefing a risk committee, the single most important lesson from this academy's ten Module 31 case studies.
