## Purpose
This exam certifies readiness to attempt Module 39's capstone projects
— it tests whether a student can integrate strategy, technical, risk,
sizing, portfolio, and psychology knowledge together, not just recall
individual modules in isolation.

## Section A — Recall (10 questions, one per cluster)
1. What is the expectancy formula? `[24.01]`
2. What is a structural stop, and why is it preferred over an arbitrary percentage stop? `[25.03]`
3. What is fixed fractional position sizing? `[26.02]`
4. What is MPT's core insight? `[27.03]`
5. What is the knowing-doing gap? `[28.01]`
6. What confirms a genuine breakout? `[13.04]`, `[14.03]`
7. What is the max loss on a naked short call? `[21.06]`
8. What does Delta measure? `[22.02]`
9. What is India VIX? `[23.02]`
10. What historical event most directly catalyzed NSE's founding? `[03.01]`, `[31.03]`

## Section B — Integrated Application (10 questions)
11. Design a complete trade: identify a hypothetical setup (strategy framework, Module 24), its stop-loss methodology (`[25.03]`), position size formula (`[26.02]`), and one psychological circuit breaker (`[28.06]`) you'd apply.
12. A trend-following trade is stopped out for the fourth time this month. Using `[24.12]`'s framework, how would you determine if this reflects normal variance or genuine edge decay?
13. Explain how India VIX, option chain OI, and market structure could together inform a same-day trading decision.
14. A trader's portfolio is "diversified" across 15 stocks, all Nifty Bank constituents. Identify the risk this creates and how you'd fix it.
15. Explain the theta decay trap and how it connects to `[28.05]`'s FOMO discussion.
16. Compare trend-following and mean-reversion's expectancy shapes — how do they differ, and why does each remain valid?
17. A trader wants to combine two strategies into a portfolio of systems. What criterion determines whether the combination genuinely diversifies risk?
18. Explain why a black swan event (`[25.07]`) requires a different defense than ordinary risk management.
19. A trader's rulebook lacks a maximum daily loss trigger. What specific psychological risk does this omission leave unaddressed?
20. Using the process-vs-outcome matrix, classify and explain: a disciplined trade, executed exactly per plan, that still lost money.

## Section C — Judgment (5 questions)
21. A trader has strong technical analysis skills but no written risk rulebook. Predict, using this academy's framework, what is most likely to eventually go wrong.
22. Evaluate: "Professional traders don't experience fear, greed, or bias." Correct this using `[28.08]`.
23. Explain why position sizing, not entry signal quality, is argued to be the "real edge" in trading.
24. A trader claims a single winning trade validates their strategy. What standard does `[24.01]` and `[29.03]` require before drawing that conclusion?
25. Reflecting on Modules 01-28 as a whole, articulate in your own words why risk management is taught before returns throughout this academy.

## Answer Key
Grade Section A directly against cited lessons. Grade Sections B and C
for INTEGRATION — does the answer connect multiple modules coherently,
not just recite one lesson in isolation.
