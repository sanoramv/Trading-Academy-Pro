# Module 02 — Market Structure

> Where financial market mechanics (Module 01) end and chart reading begins.
> This module teaches you to read the "skeleton" of price movement — trend,
> range, support/resistance, and liquidity — the vocabulary every later
> technical, Smart Money, ICT, and Wyckoff lesson will assume you already
> know cold.

## Why This Module Exists
Every advanced price-reading framework in this academy (Wyckoff, Smart
Money Concepts, ICT, classical Technical Analysis) is really just a
different lens applied to the same underlying skeleton: how price forms
highs and lows, how it holds or breaks levels, and where liquidity sits.
Learning this skeleton first, in plain language, prevents you from later
confusing "jargon from a specific school of thought" with fundamental
market truths that are common to all of them.
