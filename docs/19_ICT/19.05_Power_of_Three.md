## Core Concepts

### Definition
ICT's **Power of Three (PO3)** describes a recurring, three-stage daily
(or weekly, or session-level) price cycle: **(1) Accumulation** — a
range-bound consolidation period, typically during quieter hours,
analogous to Wyckoff's Phase B (`[17.03]`); **(2) Manipulation** — a
deliberate false move (a liquidity sweep, `[18.02]`) beyond the
accumulation range's boundary, designed to trap traders positioned in
the "wrong" direction, directly corresponding to Wyckoff's Spring/
Upthrust (`[17.05]`); and **(3) Distribution** — the genuine, sustained
directional move that follows, where the day's or session's "real"
trend actually unfolds.

### Intuition
PO3 packages an entire trading day's (or any nested timeframe's) typical
structure into the same three-phase logic already covered separately
throughout Modules 17-19 — it is essentially a compressed, single-session
application of the Accumulation/Distribution Schematic (`[17.03]`-
`[17.04]`) combined with the Market Maker Model (`[19.02]`), now framed
specifically as a repeating daily/session-level cycle rather than a
longer-term, multi-day structure.

### Mental Model
```
POWER OF THREE (PO3) — recurring daily/session cycle

STAGE 1: ACCUMULATION
  Range-bound consolidation, typically during quieter hours
  (parallels Wyckoff Phase B, [17.03])

STAGE 2: MANIPULATION
  Deliberate false move beyond the range (liquidity sweep,
  [18.02]) -- traps traders positioned wrong
  (parallels Wyckoff Spring/Upthrust, [17.05])

STAGE 3: DISTRIBUTION
  The genuine, sustained directional move -- the day's/
  session's "real" trend unfolds
  (parallels Wyckoff Phase D-E and Market Maker Model
  markup/markdown, [19.02])
```

### Visual Explanation
ICT's specific terminology choice — using "Distribution" to describe the
*genuine trend continuation* stage — is a notable departure from
Wyckoff's original usage, where "Distribution" specifically referred to
the *topping* process before a decline (`[17.04]`); ICT's PO3 framework
uses "Distribution" more generically to mean "the real move gets
distributed/expressed," applicable to either direction, a terminology
distinction worth noting to avoid confusion when moving between Wyckoff
and ICT vocabulary.

## Real World Analogy
The Power of Three is like a predictable daily rhythm at a busy market:
a quiet early setup period where vendors and buyers get organized
(accumulation), followed by a brief chaotic rush or false alarm that
sends some people scattering in the wrong direction (manipulation),
before the day's actual main business activity genuinely unfolds
(distribution) — a recurring daily pattern experienced participants
learn to anticipate rather than react to naively.

## Indian Market Example
Indian ICT-influenced Nifty 50 intraday traders specifically watch for
this three-stage cycle within the Indian trading day — an early
consolidation period, followed by a sweep of that range's high or low
(often occurring around the overlap with later global session activity),
followed by the day's genuine directional move, applying PO3 alongside
`[19.07]`'s Indian-market-adapted Kill Zone timing.

## US Market Example
US ICT day traders frequently apply PO3 specifically to the pattern
observed around the New York Open — treating the overnight/pre-market
range as "accumulation," the initial post-open volatility spike as
"manipulation," and the subsequent trend that develops through the
remainder of the New York session as "distribution."

## Historical Example
Power of Three represents ICT's specific daily/session-level compression
(popularized 2010s, per `[19.01]`) of Wyckoff's originally longer-term,
multi-day/multi-week Accumulation and Distribution Schematics (Module
17) — illustrating how ICT specifically adapted Wyckoff's structural
logic to shorter, more actively-tradeable intraday timeframes.

## Institutional Perspective
Institutional analysts note that the underlying three-stage logic
(consolidation, a clearing/shakeout event, then genuine directional
movement) has plausible grounding in how markets naturally digest
information and liquidity throughout a trading session — though PO3's
specific labeling and precise stage boundaries remain an ICT-specific
framing rather than a rigorously, independently validated academic
model.

## Retail Perspective
Retail traders should specifically practice identifying PO3's three
stages on their own traded instrument's daily chart pattern, building
empirical familiarity with how reliably (or unreliably) this specific
cycle actually manifests before relying on it heavily for live trading
decisions.

## Professional Insights
Professional analysts caution that PO3, like other ICT concepts,
risks retrospective bias if applied loosely — nearly any day's price
action can be relabeled, after the fact, as having followed
"accumulation, manipulation, distribution" — genuine, prospective
verification (documenting the expected stages before they complete,
per `[18.08]`'s retrospective-bias discussion) is essential for honest
evaluation.

## Common Mistakes
- Applying PO3 retrospectively to any day's price action without
  prospective, before-the-fact verification.
- Confusing ICT's PO3 "Distribution" (meaning the genuine trend move) with
  Wyckoff's original "Distribution" (meaning the topping process before
  decline) — these are different usages of the same word.
- Assuming PO3 completes reliably and predictably every single trading
  day without exception.

## Misconceptions
- **"PO3 is an entirely novel ICT framework."** It directly compresses
  Wyckoff's Accumulation/Distribution Schematic logic (Module 17) into a
  shorter, daily/session-level cycle.
- **"ICT's 'Distribution' stage means the same thing as Wyckoff's
  'Distribution.'** ICT uses the term more generically to mean "the real
  move gets expressed," applicable to either direction, unlike Wyckoff's
  specific top-formation usage (`[17.04]`).
- **"PO3 completes reliably every single trading day."** Like other ICT
  concepts, PO3 describes a tendency, not a guaranteed daily occurrence —
  prospective, empirical verification is essential.

## Key Takeaways
- Power of Three: Accumulation (range-bound) → Manipulation (liquidity
  sweep) → Distribution (genuine directional move).
- Directly compresses Wyckoff's Accumulation/Distribution Schematics
  (Module 17) into a shorter daily/session-level cycle.
- ICT's "Distribution" stage means something different from Wyckoff's
  original "Distribution" terminology — a notable vocabulary distinction.
- Prospective, before-the-fact verification is essential to avoid
  retrospective bias when evaluating PO3's reliability.

## Summary
Power of Three — Accumulation, Manipulation, and Distribution — compresses
Wyckoff's longer-term Accumulation/Distribution Schematic logic (Module
17) into a recurring daily/session-level cycle, combining `[18.02]`'s
liquidity sweep mechanics with `[19.02]`'s Market Maker Model structure,
while requiring careful, prospective verification to avoid retrospective
bias. This sets up the next lesson's treatment of the Judas Swing — ICT's
