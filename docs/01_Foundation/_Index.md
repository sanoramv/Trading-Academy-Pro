# Module 01 — Foundation

> The starting point for a student with zero market knowledge. By the end of
> this module you will understand what a financial market actually is, who
> participates in it, how an order you place travels through the system, and
> how to read the numbers on a live stock quote.

## Why This Module Exists
Every later module — technical analysis, options, macroeconomics, risk
management — assumes you already know how the plumbing of a market works.
Skipping this module is the single most common reason self-taught traders
misunderstand more advanced material later. Nothing here is optional.
