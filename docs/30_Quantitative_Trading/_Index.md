# Module 30 — Quantitative Trading

> Module 29 covered algorithmic execution mechanics. This module
> develops the statistical and mathematical foundations underlying
> sophisticated quant strategies: distributions, cointegration and
> pairs trading, factor investing, market making, risk models, and the
> career landscape in quant trading.

## Why This Module Exists
Quantitative trading is a distinct discipline from algorithmic
execution — it's about the mathematical and statistical *reasoning*
behind a strategy's edge, not just automating its execution. This
module gives students exposure to the concepts (statistical
foundations, cointegration, factor investing, risk models) that
underpin institutional quant trading, closing out this academy's
derivatives-through-quant progression.
