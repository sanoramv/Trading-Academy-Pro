## Section A — Recall
1. When was BSE established, and when did NSE launch?
2. What is SEBI's core mandate?
3. How are Nifty 50 and Sensex constructed (weighting methodology)?
4. What is India's current equity settlement cycle?
5. What is the difference between an FII and a DII?

## Section B — Application
6. Nifty gaps down 2% at the open following a surprise RBI rate hike. Using `[03.10]`'s framework, what should you check before assuming this is a buying opportunity?
7. You notice unusually high call OI at a strike well above the current Nifty level. What does this commonly suggest, and what should you check before relying on it?
8. A stock hits its upper circuit filter and trading halts. What does this tell you, and what should you NOT assume about the stock's next session?
9. Compare the settlement infrastructure vulnerability exploited in the Harshad Mehta scam to a settlement vulnerability that could theoretically exist today — has it been addressed?
10. Explain how a Union Budget announcement can move Nifty even before any of its provisions are actually implemented.

## Section C — Judgment
11. A trader claims "Indian markets are too manipulated to trade safely," citing the Harshad Mehta and Ketan Parekh scandals. Evaluate this claim using what you've learned about the regulatory reforms that followed each event.
12. You're evaluating an unfamiliar mid-cap stock with unusually persistent, strong buying pressure and no clear news catalyst. What historical pattern from this cluster should make you cautious, and why?

## Answer Key
1. BSE: 1875. NSE: 1992. `[03.01]`
2. Regulate, develop, and protect investors in Indian securities markets. `[03.02]`
3. Free-float market capitalization weighting. `[03.04]`
4. T+1. `[03.06]`
5. FII = foreign institutional investor; DII = domestic institutional investor. `[03.07]`
6-12. Open-ended: check whether the reaction reflects genuine policy surprise vs. uncertainty already priced in (`[03.10]`, `[24.10]`); high call OI often reflects call sellers, not bearish bias, check Chg in OI too (`[21.11]`); circuit halts pause trading, don't guarantee direction (`[03.05]`); settlement infrastructure has been substantially modernized since 1992 but ongoing vigilance remains necessary (`[31.03]`); budget provisions get priced in via anticipation and reaction to surprise vs. expectation (`[03.10]`); regulatory reform has meaningfully strengthened oversight without eliminating all risk (`[31.03]`, `[31.04]`); persistent buying with no news catalyst in illiquid names echoes the Ketan Parekh pattern (`[31.04]`).
