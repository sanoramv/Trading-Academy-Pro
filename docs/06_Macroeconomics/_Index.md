# Module 06 — Macroeconomics

> Module 05 built the vocabulary (inflation, interest rates, GDP,
> employment, policy, business cycles). This module applies that
> vocabulary at the scale central banks, global capital, and geopolitics
> actually operate on — the level where most of the "why did the market
> suddenly move" questions in this academy get answered.

## Why This Module Exists
A trader who understands demand/supply and inflation in the abstract but
has never studied how the Federal Reserve, RBI, and ECB actually make
decisions, or what quantitative easing, yield curve inversion, or global
liquidity cycles mean in practice, will still be blindsided by the events
that move markets most. This module closes that gap directly, building on
Module 05 and feeding directly into Modules 20-23 (derivatives) and 24
(strategies), where macro regime awareness matters most.
