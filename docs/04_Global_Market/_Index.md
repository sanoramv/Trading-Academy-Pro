# Module 04 — Global Market

> India does not trade in isolation. This module places Indian markets
> (Module 03) within the global system — the major exchanges, trading
> session overlaps, currency markets, and correlations that shape how
> capital flows around the world and back into India.

## Why This Module Exists
A trader who understands only Indian markets will be repeatedly surprised
by moves that originate overseas — a Fed decision, a Nasdaq selloff, a
yen carry-trade unwind — and show up in Nifty the next morning. This
module builds the global literacy needed to see those connections coming,
directly extending the FII-flow and macro-linkage themes introduced in
Module 03.
