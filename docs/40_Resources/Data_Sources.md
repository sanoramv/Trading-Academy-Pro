# Data Sources — Market Data, Charting & Research

> Where to find the live data this academy's `[32]` Daily Market
> Analysis module and `[39]` Projects require. Part of Module 40.

## Price & Chart Data
- Broker-provided charting platforms (most Indian brokers offer free charting for account holders)
- NSE/BSE official websites — delayed but authoritative price/volume data
- TradingView — widely used charting platform with free tier access

## Options Chain & Derivatives Data
- NSE's official option chain — nseindia.com, referenced throughout `[21.11]`
- Broker platforms — many provide Greeks alongside the chain, per `[22.01]`

## Volatility Indices
- India VIX — published live by NSE, `[23.02]`
- CBOE VIX — published live by CBOE, `[23.03]`

## Fundamental / Financial Statement Data
- Company annual reports and quarterly filings — directly from company investor relations pages, per `[07.09]`'s Indian annual report and `[07.10]`'s US 10-K/10-Q discussion
- SEBI/BSE/NSE corporate filings sections

## Macro Data
- RBI's Database on Indian Economy (DBIE) — rbi.org.in
- Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) — fred.stlouisfed.org
- Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) — India's official GDP/inflation data source

## A Note on Free vs. Paid Sources
Many sources above offer free tiers sufficient for this academy's
practice drills and projects (`[38]`, `[39]`) — paid, professional-
grade data (tick-level, extensive historical depth) is generally only
necessary once operating at a scale where the incremental data quality
meaningfully affects real trading decisions.
