# Recommended Books — By Topic

> A curated, topic-organized starting list, distinct from
> `Full_Book_Bibliography.md`'s complete citation index. Part of Module 40.

## Foundations & Fundamental Analysis
- Benjamin Graham — *The Intelligent Investor*
- Benjamin Graham & David Dodd — *Security Analysis*

## Technical Analysis & Price Action
- Edwards and Magee — *Technical Analysis of Stock Trends*
- Steve Nison — *Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques*

## Wyckoff, SMC & Market Structure
- Richard Wyckoff's original writings (various compilations)

## Derivatives, Options & Volatility
- Sheldon Natenberg — *Option Volatility and Pricing*

## Trading Strategy & Systems
- Michael Covel — *The Complete TurtleTrader*
- Van Tharp — *Trade Your Way to Financial Freedom*

## Risk Management & Position Sizing
- Ralph Vince — *Portfolio Management Formulas*
- Roger Lowenstein — *When Genius Failed: The Rise and Fall of Long-Term Capital Management*

## Portfolio Management
- John Bogle — *The Little Book of Common Sense Investing*

## Trading Psychology
- Mark Douglas — *Trading in the Zone*
- Daniel Kahneman — *Thinking, Fast and Slow*
- Brett Steenbarger — *The Psychology of Trading* and *Enhancing Trader Performance*

## Algorithmic & Quantitative Trading
- Marcos López de Prado — *Advances in Financial Machine Learning*
- Gregory Zuckerman — *The Man Who Solved the Market* (Renaissance Technologies)

## Market History & Behavioral Finance
- Robert Shiller — *Irrational Exuberance*
- Michael Lewis — *Flash Boys: A Wall Street Revolt*
- Nassim Nicholas Taleb — *The Black Swan*

## How to Use This List
Each book connects to specific lessons throughout this academy — check
each lesson's "Further Reading" section for the precise connection
before diving into a full book, so you know what to look for.
