# How to Keep Learning After This Academy

> Completing Modules 01-31 and their assessments is a genuine
> milestone, not an endpoint. This guide outlines how to keep growing
> from here. Part of Module 40.

## 1. Convert Knowledge into Track Record
Everything in this academy is now theory until tested against real
market conditions over time. Prioritize `[39]`'s Project 6 (paper-
trade a full quarter) if you haven't already, then extend it: paper-
trade a full year before committing meaningful real capital, tracking
expectancy (`[24.01]`) across a genuinely large sample.

## 2. Specialize Deliberately
This academy deliberately covered breadth — Foundation through
Quantitative Trading — so you could discover which area genuinely
interests you. Now pick ONE or TWO areas (per `[30.08]`'s career
landscape, if pursuing this professionally) and go deep: read the
primary sources in `Full_Book_Bibliography.md`, not just this
academy's summaries.

## 3. Re-Take Assessments Periodically
Markets and your own understanding both evolve. Re-take `[37]`'s Full
Academy Final Exam every 6-12 months — you'll likely find your answers
deepen even for questions you "passed" the first time.

## 4. Build a Community
Trading, and especially the psychological discipline `[28]` covers, is
genuinely harder to sustain in isolation. Seek out a study group, a
mentor, or a professional community (even an online one) specifically
for accountability on your `[25.08]` risk rulebook's adherence, not
just idea-sharing.

## 5. Extend Module 32 Indefinitely
`[32]`'s Daily Market Analysis module is explicitly designed as a
living document. The single highest-leverage habit from this entire
academy is continuing that daily practice — applying the full toolkit
to real, current conditions, every session, for years, not weeks.

## 6. Revisit `MASTER_SKILL.md`
This academy's teaching constitution applies to your own continued
learning, not just this curriculum: teach slowly, never skip
prerequisites, and always prioritize risk management over returns —
principles worth re-reading periodically as your own understanding matures.

## 7. Stay Current on Regulation
Markets, especially algorithmic trading rules (`[29.07]`) and
derivatives regulation, continue evolving. Build a habit of checking
`Regulator_Resources.md`'s official sources periodically, not just once during this academy.
