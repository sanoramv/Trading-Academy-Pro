# Official Regulator Resources

> Primary, authoritative sources — always prefer these over secondary
> commentary when precision matters (rule changes, current
> requirements, official data). Part of Module 40.

## India
- **SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India)** — sebi.gov.in — official circulars, regulations, investor education, and enforcement actions. Relevant to `[03.02]`, `[03.09]`, `[29.07]`.
- **RBI (Reserve Bank of India)** — rbi.org.in — monetary policy statements, MPC minutes, macroeconomic data. Relevant to `[06.01]`-`[06.10]`, `[03.10]`.
- **NSE India** — nseindia.com — official option chain data, circulars, market data, India VIX methodology. Relevant to `[21.11]`, `[23.02]`.
- **BSE India** — bseindia.com — official market data and circulars.
- **NSDL / CDSL** — official depository sites — demat account and holdings verification.

## United States
- **SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission)** — sec.gov — filings (10-K, 10-Q via EDGAR), enforcement actions, rule-making. Relevant to `[07.10]`.
- **Federal Reserve** — federalreserve.gov — FOMC statements, policy actions, economic data. Relevant to `[06.01]`-`[06.02]`.
- **CBOE (Chicago Board Options Exchange)** — cboe.com — VIX methodology, options market data. Relevant to `[23.03]`.
- **FINRA** — finra.org — broker-dealer regulation, investor alerts.

## Global / Cross-Border
- **IMF (International Monetary Fund)** — imf.org — global economic data and reports. Relevant to Module 04-06.
- **BIS (Bank for International Settlements)** — bis.org — central bank cooperation, global financial stability reports.

## How to Use This List
When a lesson references a specific rule, methodology, or historical
filing (e.g., SEBI's algo trading circulars, `[29.07]`), always verify
current status directly with the regulator's own publication —
educational content, including this academy, can become outdated as
rules evolve.
