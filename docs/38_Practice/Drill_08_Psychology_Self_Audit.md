# Drill 8 — Personal Psychology Self-Audit

> Builds `[28.01]`-`[28.08]`'s self-awareness through structured, honest reflection.

## Instructions
Answer each question honestly, in writing — this drill has no "correct"
answers, only honest ones. If you haven't traded live yet, answer based
on your best prediction of your own tendencies, and revisit this drill
after your first month of live or paper trading to check your prediction.

1. **Fear/Greed (`[28.02]`):** Describe a time (trading or otherwise) you made a decision purely because "everyone else was doing it." What would you do differently, knowing what you now know about the fear/greed cycle?
2. **Confirmation Bias (`[28.03]`):** Think of a strongly-held belief you have. Have you actively sought out the best argument AGAINST it recently? If not, do so now.
3. **Loss Aversion (`[28.03]`):** Recall a time you held onto something too long (a losing investment, a bad decision) specifically to avoid "admitting" it was wrong. What did that cost you?
4. **Tilt (`[28.04]`):** What are YOUR specific early warning signs of tilt (urge to act immediately, physical tension, a specific internal phrase you tell yourself)? Write them down explicitly.
5. **FOMO (`[28.05]`):** Describe a specific, recent situation where you felt FOMO. Did you act on it? What would your written entry criteria (`[28.06]`) have said about that specific opportunity?
6. **Discipline (`[28.06]`):** Do you currently have a written trading plan? If not, what is the single biggest obstacle to writing one this week?
7. **Resilience (`[28.07]`):** Describe your current post-loss routine, if you have one. Does it risk suppression, excessive self-criticism, or genuine process-focused resilience?
8. **Professional vs. Retail (`[28.08]`):** Which ONE behavioral practice from `[28.08]`'s framework (written plan, journaling, circuit breakers, bias countermeasures) are you currently weakest at self-administering? Commit to one specific, concrete improvement this week.

## Worked Example (Illustrative Response to Question 4)
```
"My early warning signs of tilt: (1) I start refreshing the price
chart every few seconds instead of checking periodically, (2) I catch
myself thinking 'I need to make this back NOW,' (3) I feel a specific
tightness in my chest/shoulders. When I notice ANY of these three,
my pre-committed rule is: close the trading platform for 30 minutes,
no exceptions, no negotiating with myself."
```

## Self-Check Questions
- Were you genuinely honest, or did you answer how you'd LIKE to behave rather than how you actually tend to behave?
- Did Question 8's commitment include a SPECIFIC, checkable action, or a vague intention?
- Revisit this entire drill again in one month — has anything changed?
