# Drill 1 — Chart Marking: Market Structure

> Builds `[02.01]`-`[02.08]`'s market structure skills through hands-on annotation.

## Instructions
1. Open any daily chart (Nifty, a stock, or any instrument you have access to) covering the last 3-6 months.
2. Print it or open it in an annotation tool.
3. Mark every swing high and swing low using `[02.07]`'s formal swing-point rule (a candle whose high/low is higher/lower than the N candles on either side).
4. Label each swing point sequentially: HH, HL, LH, LL per `[02.03]`'s definitions.
5. Identify and mark any BOS (trend continuation) and CHOCH (potential reversal) points per `[02.05]`.
6. Draw horizontal lines at the two or three most significant support/resistance zones per `[02.04]`.
7. Write a 2-3 sentence structural bias summary: is this instrument trending, ranging, or transitioning?

## Worked Example (Hypothetical)
```
Illustrative price sequence: 100 -> 110 (HH) -> 104 (HL) -> 118 (HH) ->
108 (HL) -> 112 (LH, fails to make new high) -> 100 (LL, BREAKS
the prior HL of 104)

MARKING:
  100 (start)
  110 = HH #1
  104 = HL #1
  118 = HH #2 (structure: still UPTREND, HH+HL confirmed)
  108 = HL #2
  112 = LH  <- first warning sign, failed to exceed 118
  100 = LL, breaks below 104 AND 108 -> CHOCH confirmed, [02.05]

STRUCTURAL BIAS SUMMARY: "The uptrend (HH/HL sequence through 118)
broke down once price failed to make a new high at 112 (LH) and then
broke the prior HL structure at 100 (LL) -- this is a CHOCH, warning
of a potential trend reversal, not yet a confirmed new downtrend
until a subsequent LH+LL sequence forms."
```

## Self-Check Questions
- Did you use a consistent, rule-based swing definition, or eyeball it?
- Does your BOS/CHOCH labeling match `[02.05]`'s exact distinction?
- Would a classmate marking the same chart identify the same swing points?
