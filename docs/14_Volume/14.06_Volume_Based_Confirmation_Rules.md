## Core Concepts

### Definition
This lesson synthesizes Module 14's five lessons into a complete
**volume-based confirmation checklist**: (1) always pair volume with
price resolution, never interpret in isolation (`[14.01]`); (2) apply
"effort versus result" analysis using spread, close position, and volume
together (`[14.02]`); (3) expect declining volume in ranges and sharp
expansion at genuine breakouts (`[14.03]`); (4) for derivatives,
distinguish volume (activity) from Open Interest (commitment) using the
four-quadrant framework (`[14.04]`); (5) recognize climactic volume
specifically as extreme magnitude combined with mature-trend context, not
high volume alone (`[14.05]`).

### Intuition
Like `[10.12]` and `[13.10]`'s module-closing syntheses, this lesson
converts five distinct volume-analysis lessons into one coherent,
repeatable process — preventing the common failure mode of learning
volume concepts individually without integrating them into consistent
practical application.

### Mental Model
```
COMPLETE VOLUME CONFIRMATION CHECKLIST (synthesizing Module 14)

STEP 1: NEVER interpret volume alone -- always pair with
        price resolution (14.01)

STEP 2: For any single candle, check EFFORT (volume) vs.
        RESULT (spread + close position) -- look for
        mismatches suggesting absorption (14.02, VSA)

STEP 3: For range/breakout analysis, confirm the EXPECTED
        pattern: declining range volume, sharp breakout
        expansion (14.03) -- treat unremarkable breakout
        volume as a fakeout warning sign (per [13.04])

STEP 4: For derivatives, ALWAYS distinguish volume from
        Open Interest -- use the four-quadrant price-OI
        framework to assess commitment durability (14.04)

STEP 5: For extreme volume events, check BOTH magnitude
        AND trend maturity before concluding a genuine
        climax/exhaustion signal is present (14.05)
```

## Indian Market Example
An Indian trader analyzing a potential Nifty 50 breakout setup would
apply this complete checklist: confirming volume pairs meaningfully with
the breakout candle's price resolution (`[14.01]`), checking for VSA-style
effort-result alignment (`[14.02]`), confirming the expected range-to-
breakout volume pattern (`[14.03]`), and if analyzing Nifty futures
specifically, checking OI alongside volume for commitment durability
(`[14.04]`) — a complete, integrated process rather than checking any
single volume concept in isolation.

## US Market Example
A US trader analyzing a potential climactic reversal in an individual
stock would apply the identical integrated process — confirming the
volume spike's magnitude is genuinely historically extreme AND occurs
after a mature trend (`[14.05]`), while cross-checking with VSA-style
effort-result analysis (`[14.02]`) and broader structural confluence
(`[10.11]`, `[11.08]`) before concluding a genuine exhaustion signal is
present.
