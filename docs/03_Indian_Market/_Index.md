# Module 03 — Indian Market

> A deep, dedicated look at the specific mechanics, history, institutions,
> and macro rhythms of Indian financial markets — essential grounding for
> any student, whether trading primarily in India or simply needing to
> understand how India fits into the global market system covered in
> Module 04.

## Why This Module Exists
Generic global market education often treats "the market" as a
US-centric default and mentions India only in passing. This module corrects
that by giving Indian market structure, history, regulation, and rhythm the
same first-principles depth this academy gives every other topic — while
also building the specific literacy (SEBI rules, NSE/BSE mechanics, T+1
settlement, Budget Day effects) that a US-only education would never cover.
