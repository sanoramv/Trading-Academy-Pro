# Module 17 — Wyckoff

> Modules 15 and 16 taught you *where* volume and time concentrate.
> Wyckoff teaches you *why* — a complete theory of how large, informed
> operators accumulate and distribute positions, and how their footprints
> appear in price and volume long before the broader public notices.

## Why This Module Exists
Richard Wyckoff's early-20th-century method is the direct historical
ancestor of most "smart money" trading concepts taught today, including
Modules 18 (Smart Money Concepts) and 19 (ICT). Understanding Wyckoff
directly, at the source, gives you the theoretical foundation those later
modules build on, and connects directly to the Composite Man concept
already previewed in `[14.05]`'s climactic volume discussion.
