## 6. Volatility & Options Context
- IV significantly elevated relative to the 20-day average — per `[24.10]`'s framework, this suggests uncertainty is already substantially priced in
- Given elevated IV and no strong directional conviction, this session favors STANDING ASIDE on a straddle/strangle bet (IV crush risk, `[22.05]`) or considering a defined-risk premium-selling structure to benefit from the expected post-event IV crush, per `[24.09]`
- PCR at 0.95, roughly neutral — no strong positioning bias evident either

## 7. Today's Plan
- **Primary decision (per `[24.10]`'s three-way framework):** given elevated pre-event IV and no strong directional conviction, choose to STAND ASIDE on any pre-announcement directional bet

- **Post-announcement plan:** reassess once the decision and tone are known; only take a directional position AFTER the initial volatility settles and a clear structural signal (per `[24.02]`/`[24.04]`) forms
- Position size: N/A for pre-event (no position taken); standard 1% risk rule applies to any post-event trade, `[26.02]`

## 8. Risk Checklist
- [x] Explicit event-driven decision made (stand aside) rather than trading through unprepared, `[24.10]`
- [x] IV crush risk explicitly considered and avoided
- [x] No position sized ahead of the event — risk exposure to the announcement itself is zero

## 9. End-of-Day Review (illustrative)

- Post-announcement, a short position was taken on the reversal below 24,150 once a clear bearish reversal candle confirmed, per `[24.04]`'s framework, closing profitably into the close
- Process check: the stand-aside decision and the post-event entry both followed the plan exactly, `[24.12]`
- Psychological note: resisting the urge to "guess" the RBI's tone ahead of the announcement (a form of `[28.05]`'s FOMO applied to event speculation) proved disciplined and correct
- Lesson reinforced: `[24.10]`'s event-driven framework's explicit three-way decision structure (directional / volatility / stand aside) prevented an emotionally-driven pre-event bet in a genuinely uncertain, already-IV-inflated setup
