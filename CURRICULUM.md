# CURRICULUM.md — Full Syllabus Map

This is the authoritative plan for every lesson in Trading-Academy-Pro. It is
the source of truth for `TODO.md` (what's left to generate) and for the
cross-reference IDs used inside lessons (`[module.lesson]`, e.g. `[10.03]`).

> order). For the *generated, linkable* table of contents — every file that
> now actually exists, with reading time, difficulty, and prerequisites —

> **[`STUDY_PLAN.md`](STUDY_PLAN.md)**. For the prioritized backlog of

> **[`ASSET_PLAN.md`](ASSET_PLAN.md)**. For every lesson's recommended
> further reading, organized and cross-linked by source type, see
> **[`REFERENCES.md`](REFERENCES.md)**.
